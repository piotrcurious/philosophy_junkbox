- Independent water supply and filtration systems.
- Private energy generation and storage facilities.
- Reinforced residential structures.
- Privately funded atmospheric conditioning infrastructure.
- Personal food production and closed-loop agricultural systems.

Such installations may continue to possess historical, technical or archival value. They do not constitute an alternative authorization to remain in a restricted territory.

The Authority further advises against attempting to establish private security jurisdictions within designated exclusion zones.

Individual survival infrastructure does not supersede collective planetary security classifications.
